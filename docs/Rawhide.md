# Rawhide — What's New (Nightlies)

These notes feed the in-app "Update Available" dialog for Rawhide / cutting-edge builds.

## Recent improvements (unreleased — ships in the next build)

- ⏱️ **Time keeps flowing in One-Shot mode** — if you use the faster "One-Shot" realism setting, the in-story clock now advances through the day like it always should have (it used to freeze on the starting time). Bonus: last-second personality corrections now stick in One-Shot mode too.
- 👥 **Group chats feel their needs and bonds just like solo chats** — characters in a group now get hungry, tired, and emotionally close on their own personal rhythm, exactly as they do one-on-one. Before, group members could drift out of sync — one character quietly soaking up everyone's mood changes, or getting hungry at the wrong rate. Regenerating, swiping, and deleting messages in a group also no longer nudge a character's feelings the wrong way.
- 🛑 **The "Cancel" button on realism actually cancels now** — hitting cancel while the realism engine is thinking stops the reply cleanly instead of pushing on, and it no longer drops a stray "evaluation interrupted" line into your chat that stuck around forever.
- 🧠 **Smarter memory on capable models** — the Journal and Growth systems no longer get needlessly knocked onto a simpler, more error-prone path just because a model politely said "nothing worth remembering this time" or the server hiccuped for a second.
- 🎭 **Expression packs work on more ComfyUI setups** — generating a full expression set through ComfyUI no longer fails outright when you haven't set up a dedicated edit workflow; it quietly uses the standard path instead.
- 🖼️ **Renaming a character keeps its face** — renaming a character no longer loses its avatar gallery, expressions, and looks (they used to get stranded and the gallery would go blank).
- 📱 **The web app heals itself after your phone sleeps** — when your phone wakes up or the connection blips, the web app now refreshes automatically instead of showing you a frozen, out-of-date chat or library. No more stuck "half-typed" ghost messages either.
- 🛡️ **Your chats are now much harder to lose** — fixed a rare but nasty bug where one hiccup while saving could silently stop *all* chat saving until you restarted the app, and an even rarer one where switching chats at exactly the wrong moment could mix up which chat got saved. Saving is now self-healing.
- 🔁 **Regenerating in group chats no longer skews your characters** — before, every "regenerate" in a group quietly stacked relationship and needs changes on top of the reply you rejected, slowly drifting bonds, moods, and needs. Now a group regen cleanly undoes the rejected reply first — exactly like 1:1 chats always did. Swiping between replies and deleting messages in groups got the same treatment.
- 💾 **Automatic backups are now bulletproof snapshots** — backups are written by the database engine itself instead of copying the file mid-write, so a backup can never be silently broken right when you need it. And restoring a backup from the recovery screen now actually reconnects everything — no more "restart required but nobody told you."
- 🗄️ **Smoother updates and rollbacks** — going back and forth between app versions (say, a nightly and a stable build) can no longer trap you on a "Database Issue Detected" screen when your data was fine all along.
- 💬 **Finding the community just got easier** — the little floating Discord icon in the sidebar is now a proper "Join the Discord" button, sitting right above "Buy Me a Coffee" (on the web app too).

- 🎛️ **Image Studio "Edit" now respects your settings — every slider actually does something** — when you tweak Steps, CFG, Sampler, Shift or Seed Mode while editing a portrait, those changes now genuinely drive the edit instead of being quietly ignored. Your Edit settings are kept **separate** from your Create settings, so dialing in an edit never messes up your normal image generation (and vice versa). New edits start on the settings that reliably work for edit models, there's a one-tap **"Use recommended"** if you ever wander off, and a gentle heads-up appears if you pick a combo that tends to come out blank. The **"How much should change?"** slider now starts stronger by default, so a change you asked for actually shows up on the first try — turn it down when you want a subtle touch-up.
- 📸 **Send your character a photo — and they actually see it** — a new 📷 attach button next to the message box lets you drop a picture straight into the chat: *"Hey, I took this beautiful photo of you — do you like it?"* If your model can see images (a vision-capable local GGUF with its mmproj loaded, or a vision model on OpenRouter/Nano-GPT), the character genuinely looks at the photo and reacts in character. The photo shows right in the chat bubble (click to zoom), works the same in group chats — every member who replies sees it — and the character quietly writes itself a one-line note about what the photo showed, so they still remember it many messages later. If your current model *can't* see images, the attach preview tells you up front with a friendly warning instead of letting the character embarrass itself. Bonus fix: vision detection now works for `.kcpps` preset users too — the app reads the model and mmproj straight out of your preset.
- 🔧 **Give any local model vision — just point it at your mmproj file** — if you have a vision GGUF with a separate mmproj (projector) file, you can now attach it from Settings → Backend (and the model settings dialog): the **Browse** button is always there under "Vision projector (mmproj)," even for models the app didn't auto-recognize as vision-capable (finetunes, renamed architectures, and models whose vision lives entirely in the mmproj). Pick the file and the badge flips to **Vision: supported** — and photo attachments start working with that model. Previously these models dead-ended on "This model can't process images" with no way to add the projector.
- 🔎 **If your model can't see the photo, the app tells you why — and offers a fix, right there** — attach a photo to a model with no vision (no mmproj, text-only API model) and instead of a mystery, the attach preview explains exactly what happened: the app checked, this model can't process images, and the character will reply without seeing it. Right below the explanation is a one-time last-resort workaround: download a small vision helper (~515 MB) that looks at photos *entirely on your machine, inside the app* — no extra programs, no cloud — and quietly passes the character a genuinely detailed description ("Reading the photo…" appears above the message box): *"Two tabby cats are sleeping on a pink blanket with two remotes in the background."* The character reacts to what's actually in the picture, in character, on the very first reply. You'll only ever see this offer in that exact moment of need — it lives nowhere else in the app; once installed, a small row in Settings → Voice & Media lets you remove it to reclaim the space.
- 🧹 **Simpler backend picker — "Pseudo-Remote" is gone, and nothing breaks** — the old separate "Pseudo-Remote" backend did the exact same thing as the local KoboldCpp backend, just launched from a `.kcpps` preset. It's now folded in: pick **Local (KoboldCpp)** and, if you want, point it at a `.kcpps` preset — same result, one fewer confusing choice. If you were using Pseudo-Remote, the app quietly moves you over on first launch (including your auto-start-on-launch preference), so there's nothing to redo.
