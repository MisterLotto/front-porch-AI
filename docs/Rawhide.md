## Recent improvements (unreleased — ships in the next build)

- 💧 **Fixed: needs would snap backwards when you regenerated a reply** — if a scene had just moved a character's Needs a lot (say a bath sending Hygiene way up), regenerating the *next* reply — or swiping between its versions — could quietly undo that change and drop the need back to where it started. The sidebar was telling the truth about a value that had silently been reverted behind the scenes. Regenerating and swiping now preserve everything your accepted messages earned, so a hard-won bath (or meal, or rest) stays banked. Applies to 1:1 and group chats and the web/mobile app.
- 🎯 **Characters' self-chosen goals are now real main quests — with steps** — when the Realism Engine decides a character genuinely wants something ("get you to admit your greatest fear", "win your heart"), that goal used to be filed away as a bare side quest you could barely see. Now, if the character doesn't already have a Primary Quest, their self-chosen goal **becomes** the main quest and immediately gets a 5-step plan they'll actively pursue, scene by scene. Already set your own main quest? Theirs politely stays a side quest — but side quests now reveal their own generated steps (tap to expand in the sidebar) instead of hiding them, and **Make primary** now truly promotes a side quest in place (it used to quietly create a duplicate and lose the quest's progress). The web/mobile app shows side quests — steps, promote, and all — for the first time too.

- 🧠 **Fixed: a character's passing obsession could follow them into your next chat** — the Realism Engine gives characters a short-lived *fixation* (a background thought they briefly dwell on — a keepsake, an argument, a worry) that's meant to surface and then fade within a single conversation. Hitting **New Chat** with the same character was quietly carrying the last conversation's fixation — and their physical stance — into the fresh start, where it colored their mood from the very first message and even got saved onto the new chat. New chats now begin with a clean slate for both, in 1:1 **and** group chats (and in the web/mobile app, which starts chats through the same engine). Bond and trust still reset to the character's baseline exactly as before — only the leftover obsession is gone.
- ✍️ **Credit where credit's due on The Stoop** — sharing a character someone else made is now a first-class thing instead of a gray area. The upload wizard has a new optional **"Original creator"** field: fill it in when a card isn't your own work and it shows as *"uploaded by you · created by them"* everywhere the card appears — the app, the phone app, and the web hub (the hub even pre-fills it from the author name embedded in the card file, and updating a card keeps its credit automatically). Leave it blank for your own creations and nothing changes.
- 🛡️ **The Stoop's house rules now have teeth about reposts (AUP v1.3)** — reposting someone else's card *without* crediting them is now a formal warnable offense: each incident is a warning delivered to your Stoop inbox, and **three warnings result in a ban**. New submissions are automatically checked against the well-known public character repositories, and uncredited look-alikes are held for a human moderator instead of slipping through. You'll be asked to re-accept the updated policy once — that's expected.
- 🧹 **Removed the built-in Chub.ai / AI Character Cards web browser** — the in-app browser that let you open Chub.ai and AI Character Cards inside Front Porch has been retired. It leaned on a heavy embedded-webview component that misbehaved on some systems (blank windows on certain Linux setups, occasional crashes on close), and it opened an unmoderated pipe into the app. You can still add characters exactly as before — the **Import** button takes any character card PNG/JSON (download them in your normal browser, then import), and **The Stoop** remains the built-in, moderated place to browse and grab community cards. This also drops a large dependency and slims the app.
- 🗂️ **Home screen sorting actually works now — and remembers your choice** — sorting your library by **Recent Activity**, **Import Date**, or **Messages Sent** was unreliable and reset every time you reopened the app. Three fixes: "Recent Activity" now follows your *last message* with each character (it used to key off when the chat was first opened, so someone you talked to today could sit at the bottom); "Import Date" now uses the real date a card was added (it used to read a number out of the image filename, so anything imported from a JSON, from Chub, or renamed just piled up as undated); and your chosen sort now visibly sticks across restarts. Same fixes apply in the web/mobile app, where "Recent" previously wasn't sorted by activity at all.
- 🏡 **The Home Screen got the warm-porch makeover too** — the library grid used to be a bit of a jigsaw: folder tags in one shade of amber, group cards ringed in purple, the grid-size slider and selection checkmarks in bright blue, and a handful of pop-up dialogs (delete confirmations, move-to-folder, the "continue a chat" picker, import progress) each in their own mismatched style that only looked right in dark mode. Everything now speaks the same cozy palette as the redesigned sidebar — one consistent card look, terracotta/honey/amber accents instead of the old neon ones, and every dialog themed the same way so it finally looks correct in **light mode** as well. Nothing moved; it just stopped clashing with itself.
- 🧭 **Lorebooks got their cockpit — import wizard, full editor, per-chat books** — the finale of the lorebook overhaul, all in the warm-porch look. **Import Lorebook** is now a proper three-step wizard (same style as Create Character): pick any lorebook file — SillyTavern, Chub, NovelAI, AgnAI, RisuAI — see exactly what's inside *before* committing (entry count, token estimate, feature chips, honest warnings), then choose where it lives: a new World, straight into one or more characters, the current group, or **this chat only** (a brand-new home — try a book in one conversation without touching your library). The **entry editor** grew Simple/Advanced tabs: the everyday fields stay simple, and everything the engine runs — conditional triggers, regex keys, placement, timers, chain rules, variety groups, per-entry overrides — is now visible and editable instead of invisible import baggage. The **sidebar** tells the truth at a glance: idle entries show a calm gray dot (red no longer means "fine but waiting"), sticky/cooldown entries show live countdowns, a token meter shows how much lore is in the prompt (and warns when the budget clipped something), and a **"would trigger next"** strip watches what you're typing and shows which lore is about to wake up. Same trigger-chance and placement controls in the web editor too.
- 🧹 **Lorebook housekeeping & fixes** — importing a character no longer creates a duplicate "X's world lore" World (existing ones are left alone, now fully yours); renaming a World finally follows through to every character and group that attached it (and no longer quietly created a second copy); and three silent data-eaters are gone: the group-settings lorebook tab now actually saves its edits, and editing entries in the world editor or group settings no longer strips the advanced settings imported books carry.

- 🧠 **Lorebooks got a real engine — full SillyTavern-grade behavior** — importing a lorebook faithfully was step one (below); now the engine actually *runs* all of it. Entries can watch a window of recent messages instead of just the last one, lore can trigger other lore in chains (with the book's own rules about what may chain), every entry lands exactly where its author placed it in the prompt — with the character info, inside the Author's Note, woven into recent chat at a chosen depth, or around the example dialogues — in the author's intended order. Timed entries work too: "sticky" lore that stays present for N messages, cooldowns so an event can't immediately repeat, and slow-burn entries that stay silent until a story is underway (these survive app restarts, per chat). "Variety groups" fire exactly one of their members at a time — great for random weather or rotating rumors — and a sensible token budget (25% of context by default) keeps giant books from crowding out your actual conversation. Identical behavior in 1:1 and group chats.
- 🐛 **Fixed: group lorebooks only worked for "always active" entries** — keyword-triggered entries in a *group's* shared lorebook (and in worlds attached to the group) never fired, ever, due to a plumbing bug: the trigger marks were being written onto throwaway copies. They trigger properly now, and the sidebar's dots tell the truth about exactly which entries are being injected — including when a variety group picks a different member than you expected.
- 📚 **Imported lorebooks finally behave the way their authors intended** — until now, importing a SillyTavern or Chub lorebook quietly mangled it: entries that were supposed to need *two* keywords together fired on just one, entries meant to appear only 25% of the time fired every time, priority numbers got misread as "stay active for 100 turns" (flooding your chats with lore), and modern regex-style trigger keys never fired at all. All of that is fixed — every setting in the book (conditional triggers, trigger chance, regex keys, timers, groups, placement, and more) is now read, stored, and honored or safely kept for upcoming engine upgrades. Japanese/Chinese/Korean trigger words work now too. NovelAI, AgnAI, and RisuAI lorebooks import as well — same formats SillyTavern accepts. **Already imported a book before this fix?** Just re-import the original file — old imports can't be repaired in place.
- 🔁 **Lorebook exports now round-trip perfectly** — exporting a World writes a genuine SillyTavern-format file, and lorebooks baked into character cards keep every setting. Curate in Front Porch, share to any other app, and nothing is lost — in either direction. Editing an imported entry in the app (or the web UI) no longer strips the advanced settings you can't see yet.
- 🪄 **The macro toolbox grew up — cards with memory and state work now** — interactive cards and lorebooks that track state actually function: `{{setvar}}`/`{{getvar}}` remember values within a chat (saved with the session), `{{setglobalvar}}` remembers across all chats, and counters (`{{incvar}}`/`{{decvar}}`) tick. Cards can also reference the conversation itself — `{{lastMessage}}`, `{{lastUserMessage}}`, `{{idle_duration}}` ("you were gone 2 hours…"), the group roster (`{{group}}`, `{{notChar}}`), and their own fields (`{{description}}`, `{{personality}}`, `{{scenario}}`, `{{persona}}`) — plus timezone clocks (`{{time::UTC+2}}`), custom date formats, `{{timeDiff}}`, `{{trim}}`, and `{{reverse}}`. Trigger keywords themselves resolve macros too, so a lorebook key of `{{user}}` fires whenever your name comes up.
- 🎲 **`{{random:sunny,rainy}}`, `{{roll:d20}}` and friends now work the way cards actually write them** — the most common macro style on Chub (single colon, comma-separated options) used to leak into the prompt as raw curly-brace text for the AI to trip over. Both separator styles now resolve everywhere macros run, including dice shorthands like `{{roll:d6}}`, `{{roll:20}}`, and `{{roll d20}}`.

- 🌐 **The Stoop opened its door to the web — hub.frontporchai.app** — the community character hub now works from any browser, no install needed. Sign in with the same account you use in the app (or create one right there, 18+ and house rules apply), browse and search the porch, upvote, follow creators, report problems, message the mod team, and share cards straight from a character PNG. You can even **poke around as a guest** — browse and download without an account at all — with a standing invite to join and share your own characters. Downloads come out as real card files: solo cards as standard V2 PNGs any compatible app can read, group casts as `.group.png` files that import into Front Porch in one tap — and the page is upfront that Realism, Needs, and group casts only come alive in Front Porch AI. The **Mod's Picks** get a proper spotlight (a featured hero + a carousel), solo characters and group casts each have their own tab so they never get mixed up, and votes and download counts tick up live while you watch — same as in the app.

- 📬 **Your Stoop inbox got sorted into two tidy piles** — "your card was approved 🎉" notices used to land in the middle of your conversation with the moderators, burying both. The inbox now has a **Notifications** tab (approvals and review notes, newest first, color-coded) and a **Moderator chat** tab that's purely the human back-and-forth — same split in the app and on the new web hub.

- ☕ **Your character keeps living while you're away (AFK)** — turn on **Dynamic Responses** (Settings → Generation) and, once you've stepped away from an open chat, your character quietly gets on with their day instead of just waiting: up to three short solitary snapshots — a meal, a nap, a shower, some reading — arrive on a timer you set (30 seconds to 5 minutes apart). Time drifts forward and their Needs move to match what they actually did — a shower lifts hygiene, a nap restores energy, a meal fills hunger — with the usual needs chip on each auto-message so you can see what changed. It never talks *to* you or breaks the scene, waits until you've traded at least one message, stops after three so it can't run away, and resets cleanly the moment you type or switch chats. Prefer to keep the engines off? It still works — you just get the quiet narration, no clock or needs. You can also flip it on straight from the message box: type **/afk** (or **/afk --messages 3 --time 5m** to send three, five minutes apart), and **/afk off** to stop.
- 📔 **Characters actually keep a diary now (especially on "thinking" models)** — The Journal was quietly writing its "Where we are" recap every chat but almost never jotting down the individual memories that make it feel alive, so most chats showed an empty diary. Two things were fixed: (1) the character is now told to capture what mattered — every promise, thing it learned about you, relationship shift, and moment that stood out — first, before the recap; and (2) on reasoning/"thinking" models (e.g. Kimi K2.6:thinking via OpenRouter/Nano-GPT), the journal update now runs with thinking switched off. Previously the model would burn its whole response budget silently "thinking" and get cut off before it ever wrote the entries — so entries appeared only intermittently (you'd have to regenerate two or three times). Now it writes them the first time, faster, and captures more of them.

- 📡 **The Stoop is alive now — upvotes and downloads tick up in real time** — when someone upvotes or downloads a character while you're browsing The Stoop, you see the number change *the moment it happens*: on the browse grid, the featured banner, an open character panel, creator profiles, even your past-downloads row. No more refreshing or hopping out and back in to see how a card is doing. Post a new character on Discord and watch the downloads roll in live.

- 💾 **Fixed: your character's Needs sometimes "rewound" after closing the app** — occasionally, closing and reopening Front Porch AI would snap the Needs bars back to where they were *before* the last reply, as if that message's changes (the little "Bladder +85, Fun +18…" chips) never happened. The app was closing a beat too fast, before that last save finished writing to disk. It now waits for every pending save to fully land before it shuts down, so the state you see when you close is exactly the state you get when you reopen.

- 🪟 **Fixed: app opening as a stuck taskbar icon you couldn't restore (Windows 11)** — if Front Porch AI ever launched to nothing but an icon on the taskbar that wouldn't maximize (and reinstalling didn't help), this is fixed. Closing the app while it was minimized could save a bogus off-screen window position that then got reloaded on the next launch, parking the window where you couldn't reach it. It now refuses to restore a window that wouldn't land on any of your monitors and recenters instead — which also repairs any install that was already stuck, and rescues windows left off-screen after unplugging a second monitor.

- 🎛️ **Your sampler settings actually reach the model now** — if you ever felt like moving Min-P, Repeat Penalty, XTC, or Dynamic Temperature did nothing… you were right. Only Temperature was truly being delivered; the rest were silently dropped on the way to the model (and Repeat Penalty was being translated into a much weaker lookalike). Every slider now arrives for real, on local models and remote APIs alike. And while we were in there, three new knobs joined the panel: **Top-P** and **Top-K** (work everywhere), and **DRY** — the modern anti-repetition sampler that catches repeated *phrases*, not just words (local models; try 0.8 if your character keeps recycling the same lines). Heads-up: because Min-P and the real Repeat Penalty now genuinely apply, replies may feel slightly different — that's the sliders finally doing their job.

- 🛋️ **The chat sidebar got a complete warm-porch makeover** — nine mismatched, bolted-on sections became three tidy groups: **🎭 Character State** (their mood, bond, trust, needs, and the time of day in one card), **📖 Journal & Memory**, and **🎲 Story Tools** (author's note, objectives, chaos, lorebook). The big fix: **Lust is no longer quarantined in its own orange box** — it's a bar right under Bond and Trust, same look, same place, in solo *and* group chats, with the refractory countdown tucked beneath it (NSFW on/off now lives in the little gear next to it). Every bar in the app finally uses one consistent style, the whole sidebar respects light mode properly for the first time, collapsed cards show a one-line vital sign ("Fond · Trusting · Evening" — with a 🧠 when they're fixated), and the sidebar remembers which groups you keep open between launches. **Author's Note sits right at the top** as its own card (with an "active · strength N" hint when one is set), flipping Realism on now pops the stats open so you instantly see what you enabled, and the **Automatic Passage of Time toggle actually works now** — it turns out it was quietly broken before this redesign too: the switch changed a value that was never saved or repainted, which is also why it seemed to snap back on (or get "forced on" after touching One-Shot Eval). It now saves properly from both the sidebar and Group Settings.
