# Rawhide — What's New (Nightlies)

These notes feed the in-app "Update Available" dialog for Rawhide / cutting-edge builds.

## Recent improvements (unreleased — ships in the next build)

- 📸 **Send your character a photo — and they actually see it** — a new 📷 attach button next to the message box lets you drop a picture straight into the chat: *"Hey, I took this beautiful photo of you — do you like it?"* If your model can see images (a vision-capable local GGUF with its mmproj loaded, or a vision model on OpenRouter/Nano-GPT), the character genuinely looks at the photo and reacts in character. The photo shows right in the chat bubble (click to zoom), works the same in group chats — every member who replies sees it — and the character quietly writes itself a one-line note about what the photo showed, so they still remember it many messages later. If your current model *can't* see images, the attach preview tells you up front with a friendly warning instead of letting the character embarrass itself. Bonus fix: vision detection now works for `.kcpps` preset users too — the app reads the model and mmproj straight out of your preset.
- 🔧 **Give any local model vision — just point it at your mmproj file** — if you have a vision GGUF with a separate mmproj (projector) file, you can now attach it from Settings → Backend (and the model settings dialog): the **Browse** button is always there under "Vision projector (mmproj)," even for models the app didn't auto-recognize as vision-capable (finetunes, renamed architectures, and models whose vision lives entirely in the mmproj). Pick the file and the badge flips to **Vision: supported** — and photo attachments start working with that model. Previously these models dead-ended on "This model can't process images" with no way to add the projector.
- 🔎 **If your model can't see the photo, the app tells you why — and offers a fix, right there** — attach a photo to a model with no vision (no mmproj, text-only API model) and instead of a mystery, the attach preview explains exactly what happened: the app checked, this model can't process images, and the character will reply without seeing it. Right below the explanation is a one-time last-resort workaround: download a small vision helper (~515 MB) that looks at photos *entirely on your machine, inside the app* — no extra programs, no cloud — and quietly passes the character a genuinely detailed description ("Reading the photo…" appears above the message box): *"Two tabby cats are sleeping on a pink blanket with two remotes in the background."* The character reacts to what's actually in the picture, in character, on the very first reply. You'll only ever see this offer in that exact moment of need — it lives nowhere else in the app; once installed, a small row in Settings → Voice & Media lets you remove it to reclaim the space.
