🏡 In the app and in your browser

The Stoop — the neighbours brought characters

A community character hub built right into the app — and open from any browser at hub.frontporchai.app. Browse featured cards, follow creators you love, vote and share, and download straight into your library. Live counters update in real time as the neighbourhood votes. Whole group casts travel too: members, lorebooks, and their living Realism state arrive intact, ready to play — and so do worlds, one file carrying a place's cover art, lore, climate and traits. Only the hub touches the network; everything else stays on your machine.

Your name opens a real profile: your picture, when you joined, your followers and lifetime stats, a bio and up to four links, with everything you've shared underneath as an art grid. Profile pictures need a confirmed email address — which also unlocks sharing your own cards.

Why people stay

Everything you need, nothing watching you

One app, out of the box. No extensions to hunt down, no servers to configure by hand.

🔒

Private by default

AI models run locally on your GPU. Offline use collects nothing and sends nothing. Remote APIs exist — but only if you turn them on.

🧠

Realism Engine

Characters build bond and trust, hold grudges, carry moods between scenes, get hungry and tired, and grow new traits as your story unfolds.

📔

Memory that lasts

The Journal keeps a real diary of the chat you're in — each memory stamped with the feeling behind it, strong ones staying vivid while quiet ones fade into the background. It all lives on your disk, and a character's own memories never leak from one chat into another.

🌍

Worlds you can build

Give a place its own climate: name the conditions, pick their emoji, set the temperature bands, choose the atmosphere and the gravity. Characters feel the heat, the cold and the thin air — and say so.

🎭

Living expressions

Portraits swap in real time with the character's emotions. Works with standard SillyTavern expression packs.

🗣️

Voice, both ways

Natural offline text-to-speech in dozens of voices, push-to-talk dictation, and a hands-free voice call mode. Bring your own Piper voice files and they sit alongside the built-in ones.

👥

One chat, a changing cast

Put a whole cast in one room. They talk to you and each other — each with their own moods, needs, and voice. And the cast is never locked: type /join to walk someone in from your library mid-scene, /exit to walk them back out, all in the same conversation.

✍️

Porch Stories

Turn your chats into an illustrated novel with a page-flip reader and audiobook narration.

🖼️

Image generation

Connect A1111, Forge, SDNext, ComfyUI, or Draw Things and get scene art and avatars without leaving the chat.

🗂️

A tidy library

Nest folders however you like, then drag characters and whole group casts into them — one at a time, or a dozen at once from the multi-select toolbar.

💾

Backed up while you work

The app snapshots its database every half hour on its own and keeps a rolling history, so a bad run of edits is a couple of clicks from rolled back. It's the database that gets saved — chats, characters and their settings — not the image files sitting on your disk. All local: there is no cloud sync to opt into.

📱

Phone & browser

The built-in web app brings your library to any browser on your network — pick up the same chat from the couch.
